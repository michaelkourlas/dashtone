# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is in reference to:

* the app "[Dashtone](https://github.com/michaelkourlas/dashtone)",
  hosted on the Google Play app store; and
* the developer "Michael Kourlas".

The purpose of this policy is to comprehensively disclose how the app accesses,
collects, uses, and shares user data.

## Definitions

This policy uses the same definitions as Google Play's [Data Safety section](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469):

* **Collected** data is data that is transmitted off the device, either to the developer or to
  a third party. Data that is only ever accessed, used, or stored on the device itself is not
  collected. (Some types of data collection are exempt from this definition; this policy does
  not rely on any such exemption.)
* Data **processed ephemerally** is accessed and used only while it is held in memory, and is
  retained for no longer than necessary to service a specific request in real time. Ephemerally
  processed data must still be disclosed, even though it isn't shown on the store listing.
* **Shared** data is data that is transferred to a third party, whether on or off the device.
  (Some types of data transfers are exempt from this definition; this policy does not rely on
  any such exemption.)

## Data access

Dashtone reads your device's music library, using the Music and audio permission, in order to display and play your music. This includes:

* metadata about the audio files stored on your device, such as song titles, artists, albums,
  genres, composers, track numbers, folder locations, and file locations; and
* the contents of any M3U playlist files found on your device.

This information is cached in a local database on your device so that it can be browsed and
played without re-reading it from the file system every time. Dashtone also stores a small
amount of additional data locally on your device, such as your app preferences and playback
state, to support its functionality.

## Data collection and sharing

Dashtone does not collect or share any user data, as those terms are defined above.

Dashtone has no internet access permission and cannot communicate with any server. Everything
described under [Data access](#data-access) is read from, and stored on, your device only --
none of it is ever transmitted off your device, to the developer, to Google, or to any other
third party. Because nothing leaves your device, none of it is processed ephemerally either:
ephemeral processing only applies to data that reaches a remote server in the first place.
